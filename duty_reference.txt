ImportEase — HS code duty/levy field reference
==============================================

The HS-code records (Firestore `hscodes` collection, produced by
import_hscodes_from_pdf.py) carry short field keys. The long names are NOT
stored anywhere in the data or code — this file is the reference.

Names are taken from the "INDICATORS FOR PREFERENTIAL RATES OF DUTY" section
and the levy definitions in the Sri Lanka Customs National Imports Tariff
Guide preamble.

PREFERENTIAL (TRADE-AGREEMENT) DUTY COLUMNS
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Stored under `preferential.<code>` on each HS code.

Code   Agreement
-----  --------------------------------------------------------------
AP     Asia-Pacific Trade Agreement (APTA)
AD     APTA — Least Developed Countries (Bangladesh, Laos)
BN     Imports from Bangladesh
GT     Global System of Trade Preferences (GSTP)
IN     Indo-Sri Lanka Free Trade Agreement (ISFTA)
PK     Pakistan-Sri Lanka Free Trade Agreement (PSFTA)
SA     SAARC countries (SAPTA)
SF     South Asian Free Trade Area (SAFTA)
SD     SAFTA — Least Developed Countries (Bangladesh, Bhutan, Maldives, Nepal)
SG     Singapore-Sri Lanka Free Trade Agreement (SLSFTA)

The full country lists per agreement live in PREFERENTIAL_AGREEMENTS in
duty_calculator.py.


NOTES ON THE PDF LAYOUT
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Header row order, left to right:
    ICL/SLSI | [Preferential Duty: AP AD BN GT IN PK SA SF SD SG] |
    Gen Duty | VAT | PAL | Cess | Excise (S.P.D) |
    [Surcharge on Customs Duty] | SSCL | S C L    (or Luxury Tax in Ch. 87)

- "S C L" is printed stacked vertically in the PDF.
- The PDF header spells it "threshhold" (double h); our field is
  `freeThreshold`.
- No single chapter contains every column. Chapters 84/85 have the full
  standard stack (everything except Luxury Tax); Chapter 87 is the only one
  with Luxury Tax (and has no SCL column).
